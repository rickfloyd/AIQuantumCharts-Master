# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

AIQuantumCharts is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle data and your rights.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

Currently, AIQuantumCharts is in early development. When operational, we may collect:

- **Usage Data**: Anonymous analytics to improve the application
- **Technical Data**: Browser type, IP address (anonymized), device information
- **User-Provided Data**: Data you explicitly choose to upload or input

### What We Don't Collect

- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not track you across other websites
- We do not collect unnecessary personal information
- We do not use your data for advertising

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Sensitive data is encrypted at rest
- Access to data is restricted and logged
- Regular security audits are performed
- Data retention follows minimal necessary principles

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Correction**: Request corrections to your data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data
- **Export**: Request data in portable format
- **Opt-out**: Refuse certain data collection

## Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

- Hosting providers
- Analytics services (with anonymization)
- Security monitoring tools

We carefully vet all third-party services for privacy compliance.

## Data Breach Protocol

In the event of a data breach:

1. We will notify affected users within 72 hours
2. We will investigate and remediate the issue
3. We will report to appropriate authorities as required
4. We will provide guidance on protective measures

## Children's Privacy

This service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## International Users

Data may be processed in different countries. We ensure adequate protection regardless of location.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via:
- Email notification (if you've provided an email)
- Prominent notice on the application
- Version history in this document

## Contact

For privacy concerns or requests:
- GitHub: [Open a privacy issue](https://github.com/rickfloyd/AIQuantumCharts-Master/issues)
- Email: Via GitHub security advisory system

## Transparency

This project is open source. You can review:
- How data is handled in the code
- Security measures implemented
- Third-party dependencies

## Compliance

We strive to comply with:
- GDPR (European Union)
- CCPA (California)
- Other applicable privacy regulations
